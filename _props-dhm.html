YOU ARE NOT STEVE MAREK

You woke up in his body eleven days ago. His wife has not noticed. His neighbours have not noticed. You would like to find out what else they would not notice.
Every night, change one thing in somebody’s house.
TOO SIMILAR and they walk straight past it. Nothing happens.
TOO DIFFERENT and they are certain something is wrong. That is Suspicion, and Suspicion is how you lose.
IN BETWEEN and they doubt themselves. That is the entire game.
The scanner will tell you which one you are about to cause, before you buy it. It does not lie.
